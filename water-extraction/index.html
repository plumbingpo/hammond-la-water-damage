Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Hammond Restoration Co provides emergency water extraction throughout Hammond using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Why Professional Water Extraction Matters

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Professional truck-mounted extraction equipment removes water hundreds of times faster than consumer tools, so what might take you an entire day with a shop vacuum takes our equipment minutes. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. The payoff is a shorter drying period, lower equipment cost and, most important of all, less risk of mold growing unseen in trapped moisture.

When to Call for Water Extraction in Hammond

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Storm water collecting in the lowest level of the home
  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Store a bucket and spare towels where you can grab them, along with the number for a restoration company
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age
  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early
  • Keep valuables and stored boxes off the basement floor on shelves or pallets
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season so you know it will run
